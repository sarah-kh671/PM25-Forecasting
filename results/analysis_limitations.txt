GENERAL DATA EXPLORATION - ANALYSIS LIMITATIONS

This script calculates descriptive statistics, missing values, exact duplicate rows, IQR potential outliers, histograms, boxplots, Pearson and Spearman correlations, and PM2.5 threshold counts.

The following analyses are not calculated here: Isolation Forest, Local Outlier Factor (LOF), VIF, statistical p-values, ACF, PACF, ADF stationarity tests, sentinel-value validation, and full data lineage/leakage verification. These should remain unreported until they are completed and verified.

IQR flags identify unusual values; they do not prove that values are incorrect and do not justify automatic deletion.
